## What I work on

- **Business systems:** ERP, point of sale, inventory, sales and operational management
- **Web applications:** planning and building products around real user and organisational needs
- **Process improvement:** translating day-to-day challenges into clearer, more reliable workflows

- **SmartDrive MZ** — driving-school management platform covering students, instructors, vehicles, lessons, payments and learning progress.
